Privacy policy

Eegai Technologies, Tamil Nadu, India. Last updated: September 2026.

This policy covers every browser extension listed on this website.

What we collect

For extensions with a free trial and licence, we store your email address, a device ID generated from your computer, your trial start date, your licence status and, if you pay, the UPI reference number (UTR) and amount. We use this only to run the trial and licence.

What stays on your computer

Portal logins you save, your settings, and files you upload are stored and processed inside your browser. They are not sent to Eegai. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Google data (Gmail Summary)

Eegai Gmail Summary reads the sender, subject and received time of your mails through Google's Gmail API and writes them to a Google Sheet that you own. This data goes directly between your browser and Google. Eegai does not receive or store it. Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track the websites you visit.

Deleting your data
